Research Templates

Simple templates to help you organize different stages of a research project.

Research Proposal Template

Use this structure to organize the main parts of a research proposal.

Research Topic: Research Problem: Research Questions: Research Objectives: Literature Review: Research Gap: Research Method: Data Sources: Expected Contribution: Research Limitations:

Literature Review Matrix

Use this template to compare studies and organize important findings.

Paper / Study Title: Author & Year: Research Method: Research Context: Key Finding: Research Limitation: Potential Research Gap: Relevance to My Research:

Research Planning Template

Use this template when developing a new research idea.

Research Topic: Research Problem: Main Research Question: Research Objectives: Target Population / Context: Possible Research Method: Possible Data Sources: Expected Outcome: Potential Contribution:

Research Gap Worksheet

Use this worksheet to organize observations from previous research.

Research Topic: What has previous research studied? What methods have previous studies used? What limitations have I noticed? What areas appear under-explored? What evidence supports the potential gap? How could my research address this area?

Research Paper Outline

Use this basic structure to organize a research paper.

1. Introduction 2. Literature Review 3. Research Method 4. Results / Findings 5. Discussion 6. Conclusion 7. References
These templates are general educational resources. Adapt them to the requirements of your university, discipline, journal, supervisor, or research project.
