About Research Toolkit

Research Toolkit is a simple, beginner-friendly resource designed to help students organize and plan different stages of academic research.

Why This Project?

Starting research can be difficult when students are unsure how to develop research questions, organize previous studies, identify potential research gaps, choose a research method, or structure a research proposal.

Research Toolkit was created to provide simple tools and templates that can help beginners approach these early research tasks in a more organized way.

Creator

Sneha Yadav created Research Toolkit as an independent digital project focused on making basic research planning and organization more accessible to beginner researchers.

The project combines research interests with practical digital problem-solving and is being developed as an evolving educational resource.

Important Note

Research Toolkit provides general educational guidance and templates. It does not replace academic supervision, institutional guidelines, ethical review requirements, or discipline-specific research methods.

Users should verify research decisions, sources, citations, and methodology according to the requirements of their institution, discipline, journal, or supervisor.
